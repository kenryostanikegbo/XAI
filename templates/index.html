Model Card

Two classifiers were trained on the OULAD integrated matrix (22,311 student-presentation rows, 44 one-hot columns): Logistic Regression with L2 regularisation, and a Random Forest with 500 trees. The held-out test set has 6,732 rows at the natural 31.4 per cent Fail prevalence. F1 was the tuning metric; six metrics are reported below.
